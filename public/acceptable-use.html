Acceptable Use Policy

Rules intended to keep WyBuild useful and safe for developers.

Allowed

Normal software development, testing, packaging, release preparation, CI/CD experimentation and legitimate personal or commercial applications.

Not allowed

Malware, credential theft, phishing, destructive payloads, unauthorized access, cryptocurrency mining, abuse of GitHub Actions, evasion of security controls, or workloads designed to exhaust shared infrastructure.

Enforcement

WyBuild may suspend abusive activity or accounts where reasonably necessary to protect users, GitHub resources and service infrastructure.
