Billing & Refunds

Billing information for WyBuild Pro.

Prices

The current Pro price is shown in USD with a displayed NGN equivalent. The NGN figure is a display equivalent; the payment currency used by the configured Flutterwave checkout is shown before payment.

Renewals

Pro subscriptions may renew according to the selected billing cycle until auto-renewal is cancelled. Cancellation stops future renewal while already-paid access remains active through its paid period.

Refund requests

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact the service operator with the transaction reference and account login. Refund decisions are handled case-by-case and subject to the payment provider's rules and applicable law.

Payment security

Raw card credentials are not intended to be stored by WyBuild. Payment authorization and verification are performed through Flutterwave.
