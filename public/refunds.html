Billing & Refunds

Billing information for WyBuild one-time AI credit packs.

Prices

Credit-pack prices are shown in USD with a displayed NGN equivalent. NGN values are display equivalents; the configured Flutterwave checkout charges the stated payment currency before purchase. Credit purchases are one-time and do not automatically renew.

Renewals

Credit packs do not renew automatically. Buy another pack only when you need more credits.

Refund requests

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact the service operator with the transaction reference and account login. Refund decisions are handled case-by-case and subject to the payment provider's rules and applicable law.

Payment security

Raw card credentials are not intended to be stored by WyBuild. Payment authorization and verification are performed through Flutterwave.
